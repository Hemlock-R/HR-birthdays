App Guide & Instructions

What HR-birthdays is about

HR-birthdays is your complete team and personal tracking system designed to ensure you never miss a celebration. It handles countdowns, personal greetings, and group event coordination.

How to Navigate

Use the category filter pills (All, Personal, Work, Church, Team, Management) below the search bar to sort entries instantly, or type a name into the search box to find someone right away.

Button Guide

  • Add Birthday: Opens the form to add a new person with phone, gender, age group, and category.
  • Wish (on Today's Banner): Instantly opens WhatsApp with a pre-written, tailored birthday message for that individual.
  • Announce to [Category]: Formulates a group broadcast alert including venue and time details to share with your organization.
  • Share (on Cards): Shares a reminder or upcoming update to people other than the birthday celebrant
  • Edit / Delete Icons: Modify or remove existing birthday entries from your local tracker.

Optional Venue & Time Details

When creating or editing a non-personal birthday (like Church, Work, Team, or Management), you can optionally add a celebration location and time. These details automatically append to your group announcements so everyone knows where and when to gather!
